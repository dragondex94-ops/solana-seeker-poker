Privacy Policy — Solana Seeker Poker

Effective Date: November 5, 2026
Application Package: com.solanaseeker.poker

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (No PII)

Solana Seeker Poker does not collect, store, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contact list, camera, microphone, or precise device geolocation.

2. Non-Custodial Wallet Interaction (Solana MWA 2.0)

When you choose to connect your wallet using the official Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter 2.0 (Seed Vault), the application receives only your public Solana wallet address and optional account label. We never request, access, or have visibility into your private keys, seed phrases, or biometric credentials.

3. Local Storage & Multiplayer Privacy

Game progress (virtual chip balance, experience points XP, unlocked cosmetics, and daily timers) is stored locally on your device via standard Android WebView localStorage. During live multiplayer table synchronization, only a temporary session ID and short display nickname are transmitted; full wallet addresses and private hole cards (prior to Showdown) are never broadcast to other players.

4. Public Blockchain Transactions

Optional purchases of virtual in-game items signed by you on Solana Mainnet (in SOL, USDC, or $SKR) are recorded on the public Solana blockchain by nature of decentralized ledger technology.

5. Contact & Support

For any privacy-related inquiries regarding Solana Seeker Poker, please contact the developer via the official Solana dApp Store publisher listing.
