Privacy Policy — Solana Seeker Poker

Effective Date: November 16, 2026
Application Package: com.solanaseeker.poker

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (No PII)

Solana Seeker Poker does not collect, store, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, contact list, camera, microphone, or precise device geolocation.

2. Non-Custodial Wallet Interaction (Solana MWA 2.0)

When you choose to connect your wallet using the official Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter 2.0 (Seed Vault), the application receives only your public Solana wallet address and optional account label. We never request, access, or have visibility into your private keys, seed phrases, or biometric credentials.

3. Local Storage, Cloud Sync (Supabase) & Multiplayer Privacy

Local preferences and guest progress (virtual chip balance, experience points XP, Seeker Pass Season 1 progression, and daily check-in timers) are stored locally on your device via standard browser/WebView localStorage. When you connect a wallet, your public wallet address and virtual club stats (virtual chips, XP, Cup Tickets, VIP Pass status, daily streak timers, and Sunday Cup scores) are synced with our cloud database (Supabase) for cross-device profile restoration and public leaderboards. During live multiplayer table presence via WebSocket relays, only a temporary session ID and display nickname are shared; private hole cards (prior to Showdown) are never broadcast to other players.

4. Public Blockchain Transactions

Optional purchases of virtual in-game items or the seasonal Seeker Pass signed by you on Solana Mainnet (in SOL, USDC, or $SKR) are recorded on the public Solana blockchain by nature of decentralized ledger technology and can be viewed via public blockchain explorers such as Solscan.

5. Contact & Support

For any privacy-related inquiries regarding Solana Seeker Poker, please contact the developer via the official Solana dApp Store publisher listing or website (https://seekerclub.app).
